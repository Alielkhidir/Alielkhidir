## About

Junior SOC analyst focused on threat detection, log analysis, and incident response. I'm ISC2 CC certified and hold the Google Cybersecurity and IBM Cybersecurity Analyst professional certificates.

Before cybersecurity, I built and ran my own trading business, which taught me to work independently and stay accountable without supervision.

I publish my labs here to show how I think and learn. I'm open to entry-level SOC and security analyst roles, remote included.
